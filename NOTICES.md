  Source: [aruljohn/Bible-kjv](https://github.com/aruljohn/Bible-kjv).

## Strong's numbers (KJV+)

- **KJV word→Strong's tagging** — from
  [kaiserlik/kjv](https://github.com/kaiserlik/kjv). KJV text is Public Domain;
  Strong's numbering is from Strong's Exhaustive Concordance (1890), Public Domain.
- **Strong's Hebrew & Greek dictionaries** (definitions) — from
  [OpenScriptures/strongs](https://github.com/openscriptures/strongs),
  licensed **CC BY-SA**. Derived from James Strong's 1890 concordance.
  "Strong's dictionary data from OpenScriptures, used under CC BY-SA."
